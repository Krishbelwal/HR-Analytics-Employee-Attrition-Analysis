Business problem -  

Our client, a mid-sized company, is facing challenges with employee turnover and wants to understand the key reasons why employees leave. They have historical HR data containing employee demographics, job roles, compensation, performance, and engagement metrics.

The company wants a data-driven solution to help HR answer these questions:


1. Which employees are at risk of leaving the company - age group, gender, department, job role, job satisfaction
2. How do career growth factors like years at company, years in current role, and promotions impact employee retention



## Business Keywords

- **Employee Turnover:** The rate at which employees join and leave an organisation during a specific period.  

- **Employee Retention:** The organisation’s ability to keep employees for a longer period and reduce attrition.  

- **Employee Engagement Metrics:** Measures used to evaluate employee interest, involvement, motivation, commitment, and satisfaction with their work.
